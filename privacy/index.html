Important platform status

The public Creator Hub is currently an informational GitHub Pages site marked as in development. The reviewed public frontend does not currently offer account registration, Google sign in, subscriptions, payments, uploads, creator profiles, forums, verification, analytics or advertising. References to those functions below describe planned processing and do not mean that the function is live. This policy will be updated before materially different processing begins.

01

Scope and current status

This policy applies to personal information handled by NovaX Studios Ltd. through creators.novaxstudios.art, related Creator Hub communications and future Creator Hub accounts or services when they are launched. It does not replace the privacy notices of external websites linked by creators or service providers acting as independent controllers.

Active now Public development site

Static hosting, ordinary network requests, contact by email and delivery of the Supabase browser library through jsDelivr.

Planned Creator platform features

Accounts, profiles, posts, forums, subscriptions, handle services, verification, analytics and account management tools.

03

Personal information covered by this policy

Information used by the current public site

  • IP address and request details processed by GitHub Pages for hosting and security.
  • IP address, browser type, browser version, referring domain, requested CDN URL, request time, device identifiers and diagnostic data processed when jsDelivr delivers the Supabase JavaScript library.
  • Information included in emails or other correspondence sent to NovaX.

Information planned for Creator Hub accounts

Private account information

Email address, internal user ID, authentication provider ID, session and security information, subscription status, transaction references, support messages, moderation records and relevant technical logs.

Public profile information

Username, handle, display name, profile picture, banner, biography, social links, verification badge, public announcements, posts, comments and merchandise showcases chosen for publication.

Optional information

Profile media, biography, social links and other fields clearly marked optional. A user should not publish information that they want kept private.

Information that may be required

Email address, account identifier and a unique username or handle may be required to create and secure an account. Any other required field will be identified when collected.

Additional categories may include:

  • name and profile picture received from Google if Google sign in is introduced;
  • posts, comments, announcements, reports and forum contributions;
  • billing name, billing contact details, subscription history and payment references where paid services are launched;
  • IP addresses, browser details, device data, sign in events and security logs;
  • usage and performance information where analytics is implemented with appropriate notice;
  • evidence used to verify identity, creator status or ownership of a social account where verification is introduced; and
  • guardian contact, authority and consent records for eligible users aged 16 or 17 where required.

NovaX does not receive a user's Google password. Users must not send passwords, payment card details, identity documents or security codes by ordinary email unless NovaX provides a specifically identified secure process and explains why the information is required.

Information may be collected:

  • directly from a person who emails or otherwise contacts NovaX;
  • automatically through ordinary hosting and CDN network requests;
  • in the future, through account registration, Google sign in, profile editing, image uploads, posts, comments, support requests and account settings;
  • in the future, from a payment provider, limited to transaction, billing and subscription information required to administer a purchase;

  • through browser storage or similar technology used for essential authentication and security, and through optional analytics only if introduced with the required choice and notice.

07

Providers and infrastructure

Active GitHub Pages

Hosts the public static website. GitHub states that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Active jsDelivr

Delivers the Supabase browser library. CDN requests can include IP address, browser details, referring domain, requested URL, request time and diagnostic data. See the jsDelivr Privacy Policy.

Configured, not a live account service Supabase

The public code configures a Supabase client. Supabase is intended to provide authentication, database and potentially file storage for future features. See the Supabase Privacy Policy.

Planned Google

May provide sign in if OAuth is enabled. The reviewed public frontend does not contain a Google sign in control or confirmed scopes. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Supabase processing

If account features launch, Supabase may process account IDs, email addresses, authentication events, session data, profile records, posts, comments, subscription status, moderation data, verification records and uploaded files according to the features actually enabled. NovaX would act as controller for Creator Hub purposes, while Supabase would generally process platform data as a service provider under its applicable terms and data processing arrangements.

Supabase states that a project's database, Auth service, Storage objects and backups are hosted in the AWS region selected when the project is created, while some supporting processing may occur elsewhere. The region for the configured Creator Hub project has not been verified from the repository. NovaX must confirm and record that region before launching account processing.

Public profile files may be deliberately made publicly retrievable. Private or restricted uploads must use suitable storage access rules and signed access mechanisms. Storage configuration has not been verified and no private upload feature is currently live.

08

Google sign in

Not currently available on the reviewed public site

If Google sign in is introduced, Google may provide a stable account identifier, email address, email verification status, name and profile picture, depending on the scopes shown on the consent screen. NovaX would use that information to create or link a Creator Hub account, authenticate the user and display profile information only where the user chooses to make it public.

Google handles the user's Google credentials. NovaX Creator Hub and Supabase do not receive the user's Google password. NovaX will not request access to Google Drive, Gmail, contacts or another Google service unless a separate feature genuinely needs it and the user is given a clear scope request and updated privacy information first.

Supabase may process the resulting provider identity, tokens and session records for authentication. Google remains responsible for its own processing under its privacy terms. Removing Google access does not automatically delete information previously supplied to Creator Hub; a separate deletion request may still be required.

Payments and subscriptions

Planned, not currently available

Creator Hub may offer subscriptions and handle protection services in the future. No payment processor or checkout flow has been confirmed in the reviewed code. Before paid services launch, this policy and the checkout notice will identify the processor, the information it receives, applicable international transfers and the information retained by NovaX.

Sharing and public disclosure

Information may be disclosed:

  • to GitHub Pages, jsDelivr and Supabase for the roles described above;
  • to Google if sign in is enabled and selected by the user;
  • to a confirmed payment provider if paid services launch;
  • to support, security, moderation, professional adviser or verification providers where needed and contractually appropriate;
  • to courts, regulators, law enforcement or other authorities where disclosure is required or lawfully requested;
  • to a buyer, investor or adviser during a genuine business reorganisation, subject to appropriate safeguards; and
  • to the public when a user intentionally publishes profile information or content.

NovaX does not sell Creator Hub personal information to advertisers. This does not mean that information is never shared. Public content is visible to anyone and can be copied, indexed or shared by others outside NovaX's control.

11

Retention and deletion

NovaX retains information only for as long as reasonably needed for the stated purpose, account administration, security, dispute handling and legal obligations. Exact schedules for planned account features have not yet been approved and will be documented before launch.

  • Active accounts: core account and profile records would normally remain while the account is active.
  • Deleted accounts: information would be deleted or de-identified after closure, subject to backup cycles, fraud prevention, disputes and legal retention duties.
  • Public content: content under NovaX's control would normally be removed or de-identified following deletion, but copies, quotations, search caches and third-party reposts may remain.
  • Backups: residual copies may remain until overwritten under the applicable provider backup cycle and would not be restored for ordinary use.
  • Security logs: retained according to the risk, investigation need and provider schedule.
  • Billing records: retained for the statutory period applicable to the particular accounting, tax or consumer record.
  • Support correspondence: retained for as long as needed to resolve the request and maintain appropriate business records.
  • Verification records: limited to what is necessary to evidence a decision, prevent impersonation and meet legal requirements.

Until an account deletion tool is implemented, a person may request deletion by emailing contact@novaxstudios.art. NovaX may verify identity before deleting or disclosing account information.

12

Cookies, browser storage and analytics

The reviewed static site does not include a first-party analytics or advertising tool. It loads a Supabase browser client from jsDelivr, but no live sign in control is present. Ordinary network requests still disclose technical information to the hosting and CDN providers.

If authentication launches, Supabase may use browser storage or cookies to keep a user signed in, refresh authentication sessions and protect the account. Those technologies would be treated as essential where strictly necessary for the service and security. The repository contains a Next.js session refresh proxy, but GitHub Pages does not execute that server code. It must not be treated as an active security control for the current static site.

NovaX will not introduce non-essential analytics, advertising or marketing storage without updating this notice and obtaining consent where PECR or another applicable law requires it. Any consent tool must allow refusal and withdrawal as easily as acceptance.

13

Children and younger creators

Account safeguards must be completed before launch

The planned account service is intended for creators aged 16 or over. A creator aged 16 or 17 may require a parent or legal guardian to participate in verification, permissions and contracting. The exact process is not yet implemented.

Guardian involvement does not by itself resolve every data protection, contract or online safety requirement. Before allowing younger creators to register, NovaX must complete appropriate risk and impact assessments, decide how age and guardian authority will be established, apply high privacy settings by default, minimise collection and provide clear information suitable for younger users.

Where processing relies on consent for an online service offered directly to a child, the applicable age and parental authorisation rules may differ between the United Kingdom and EU member states. NovaX will apply the rule relevant to the user and processing activity. Users below the permitted age must not create an account or misstate their age.

14

Security and personal data breaches

NovaX uses or intends to use measures proportionate to the feature and risk, including HTTPS, authentication controls, access restriction, provider encryption, session management, logging, backups and tested database permissions. Supabase states that customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest. NovaX does not claim that Row Level Security, private storage rules, administrator roles or monitoring are operational until they are configured and tested for the deployed application.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their sign in method, device and recovery information, and should report suspected unauthorised access promptly.

NovaX will assess suspected personal data breaches, contain and document them, notify affected providers, and report to the ICO or another competent authority within the legally required period where the reporting threshold is met. Affected individuals will be informed where the law requires it.

15

International transfers and external links

GitHub, jsDelivr, Supabase and future providers may process information in more than one country. NovaX will use an applicable adequacy regulation or decision, approved contractual clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or another lawful transfer mechanism where a restricted transfer occurs. Additional technical and organisational safeguards will be considered according to the risk.

The exact Supabase project region remains pending confirmation. Supabase states that core project data stays in the selected region, while limited supporting processing may occur elsewhere through its subprocessors. NovaX will record the selected region and review the relevant transfer documents before account data is placed into production.

Creator profiles may link to social networks, shops and other external websites. Those services are controlled by their respective operators and use personal information under their own policies. NovaX is not responsible for an external site's privacy practices merely because a link appears on Creator Hub.

16

Your privacy rights

Depending on the applicable law, lawful basis and circumstances, a person may have rights to:

  • request access to personal information and information about how it is used;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • request erasure;
  • restrict processing;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests and object to direct marketing at any time;
  • receive certain information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time where consent is the basis, without affecting earlier lawful processing; and
  • challenge a qualifying decision based solely on automated processing.

Rights are not absolute and exemptions may apply. To submit a request, email contact@novaxstudios.art. NovaX may request proportionate information to confirm identity and authority before releasing, changing or deleting sensitive account information. Requests will be handled within the period required by applicable law.

17

Automated decision making

The reviewed public site does not make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects. Verification badges, moderation outcomes and paid privileges are not currently live. If NovaX later introduces qualifying automated decisions, it will provide the required explanation, safeguards and review rights before using them.

Changes, questions and complaints

Questions or complaints should first be sent to contact@novaxstudios.art. Individuals may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.
