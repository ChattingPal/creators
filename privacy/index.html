08

Google and Discord sign in

Google

Configured and successfully tested

Google OAuth is available from the public sign-in and signup pages. Google may provide a stable account identifier, email address, email verification status, name and profile picture through the ordinary sign-in scopes shown on the consent screen. NovaX uses that information to create or link an authentication account and authenticate the user. No sensitive Google scopes are requested.

Google handles the user's Google credentials. NovaX Creator Hub and Supabase do not receive the user's Google password. NovaX will not request access to Google Drive, Gmail, contacts or another Google service unless a separate feature genuinely needs it and the user is given a clear scope request and updated privacy information first.

Discord

Available through Supabase OAuth

Discord OAuth is available from the public sign-in and signup pages. NovaX requests only the permissions needed to receive the user's Discord identity and email address. NovaX does not request access to Discord servers, messages, connections, friends, activities, contacts, voice data or other Discord account content.

Discord handles the user's Discord credentials. NovaX Creator Hub and Supabase do not receive the user's Discord password. Supabase processes the provider identity, tokens and session records required to complete authentication. NovaX does not use those tokens to access broader Discord data.

Google and Discord remain responsible for their own processing under their respective privacy terms. Removing provider access does not automatically delete information previously supplied to Creator Hub. A separate account deletion request may still be required.

11

Retention and deletion

NovaX retains information only for as long as reasonably needed for the stated purpose, account administration, security, dispute handling and legal obligations. Exact schedules for planned account features have not yet been approved and will be documented before launch.

  • Authentication records: user identifiers, provider identity, session and security records are retained while authorised access remains active and afterwards only for the period needed for security, disputes, legal duties and provider backup cycles.
  • Deleted accounts: information would be deleted or de-identified after closure, subject to backup cycles, fraud prevention, disputes and legal retention duties.
  • Public content: content under NovaX's control would normally be removed or de-identified following deletion, but copies, quotations, search caches and third-party reposts may remain.
  • Backups: residual copies may remain until overwritten under the applicable provider backup cycle and would not be restored for ordinary use.
  • Security logs: retained according to the risk, investigation need and provider schedule.
  • Billing records: retained for the statutory period applicable to the particular accounting, tax or consumer record.
  • Support correspondence: retained for as long as needed to resolve the request and maintain appropriate business records.
  • Verification records: limited to what is necessary to evidence a decision, prevent impersonation and meet legal requirements.

Until an account deletion tool is implemented, a person may request deletion by emailing contact@novaxstudios.art. NovaX may verify identity before deleting or disclosing account information.

12

Cookies, browser storage and analytics

The reviewed static site does not include a first-party analytics or advertising tool. The public sign-in and signup pages load the Supabase browser client from jsDelivr. Ordinary network requests still disclose technical information to the hosting and CDN providers.

The public authentication and account pages use Supabase's supported browser storage to retain session credentials in the browser. This allows a signed-in session to continue across page reloads and tabs in the same browser profile until the user signs out, the session expires or the stored browser data is removed. Entered passwords, including a new password submitted through the reset password page, are not displayed, written to browser storage, logged by the page or placed in the page URL. Supabase receives the password directly over HTTPS and manages the authentication record. The repository also contains a Next.js session refresh proxy, but GitHub Pages does not execute that server code. It must not be treated as an active control for the current static frontend.

NovaX will not introduce non-essential analytics, advertising or marketing storage without updating this notice and obtaining consent where PECR or another applicable law requires it. Any consent tool must allow refusal and withdrawal as easily as acceptance.

13

Children and younger creators

Account safeguards must be completed before launch

Basic authentication accounts are intended for users aged 16 or over. Creating an authentication account does not mean that a person has been accepted for creator services. A creator aged 16 or 17 may require a parent or legal guardian to participate in verification, permissions and contracting before any creator, financial or legal service is enabled. That process is not yet implemented.

Guardian involvement does not by itself resolve every data protection, contract or online safety requirement. Before enabling creator services for younger users, NovaX must complete appropriate risk and impact assessments, decide how age and guardian authority will be established, apply high privacy settings by default, minimise collection and provide clear information suitable for younger users.

Where processing relies on consent for an online service offered directly to a child, the applicable age and parental authorisation rules may differ between the United Kingdom and EU member states. NovaX will apply the rule relevant to the user and processing activity. Users below the permitted age must not create an account or misstate their age.

14

Security and personal data breaches

NovaX uses measures proportionate to the operational authentication service, including HTTPS, Supabase authentication and tested session handling. Supabase states that customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Additional controls may be introduced for later features. NovaX does not claim that Row Level Security, private storage rules, administrator roles, monitoring or other controls are operational until they are configured and tested for the deployed application.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their sign in method, device and recovery information, and should report suspected unauthorised access promptly.

NovaX will assess suspected personal data breaches, contain and document them, notify affected providers, and report to the ICO or another competent authority within the legally required period where the reporting threshold is met. Affected individuals will be informed where the law requires it.

Your privacy rights

Depending on the applicable law, lawful basis and circumstances, a person may have rights to:

  • request access to personal information and information about how it is used;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • request erasure;
  • restrict processing;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests and object to direct marketing at any time;
  • receive certain information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time where consent is the basis, without affecting earlier lawful processing; and
  • challenge a qualifying decision based solely on automated processing.

Rights are not absolute and exemptions may apply. To submit a request, email contact@novaxstudios.art. NovaX may request proportionate information to confirm identity and authority before releasing, changing or deleting sensitive account information. Requests will be handled within the period required by applicable law.
